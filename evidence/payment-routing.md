# Payment Routing — Northern Cyprus (TRNC)

Per master prompt §6.2. Location confirmed by owner 2026-10-30: Kyrenia, Northern Cyprus (TRNC).

## Why this needs care

The Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus is recognized as a sovereign state only by Turkey. It is **not** part of the Republic of Cyprus's EU/SWIFT-connected banking system, and most TRNC residents bank through Turkish-system banks (TRNC uses the Turkish Lira). This matters because several payment platforms list "Cyprus" as supported without distinguishing which Cyprus they mean — and the difference is the whole ballgame for whether a payout actually lands.
