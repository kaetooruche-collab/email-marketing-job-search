## Method (audit-first, not service-first)

Don't pitch "email marketing services." Find a business with an **observable, specific gap**, name it precisely, and open with the gap — not the pitch.

Qualifying signal checklist (need at least one, concrete and visible, not assumed):
- Has a website/social presence collecting emails but no visible newsletter or follow-up sequence
- Runs webinars/events with no visible registration-to-attendance email flow
- E-commerce store with no visible abandoned-cart or post-purchase sequence
- Newsletter exists but is discount-only / low-effort (no lifecycle structure)
- Advertises a lead magnet with no visible nurture after opt-in

First message principle: point at the one gap, propose the smallest fix, no agency pitch, no multi-service menu. E.g. "I noticed [X] — I'd set up a 3-email flow to [specific outcome] instead." This matches master prompt §12.3 ("one primary offer per prospect," "don't send a generic list of every service").

Channel priority, per master prompt §12/§7.3:
1. Warm introduction (person you or the business owner already trusts) — highest conversion, skips credibility-from-zero problem
2. Direct, specific, personalized outreach to an identified decision-maker with an identified gap — never mass/generic
3. Platform-based cold outreach (LinkedIn 1st-degree connections who run businesses) — only after 1 and 2 are exhausted

Explicitly ruled out per master prompt §2.1/§8.2/§18: free full audits/strategies as a sales tactic, substantial unpaid trial work, mass unsolicited DMs, scraping private contact info.
